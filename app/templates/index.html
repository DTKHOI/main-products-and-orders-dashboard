Academic Student Project

About ProductFlow: ProductFlow is a modern, full-stack web application developed as an academic capstone project by an Information Technology student. It is engineered to simulate DOJI-level or real-world inventory tracking, multi-role authorization, and order management workflows

Academic Notice: This is a personal academic learning project created solely for educational purposes and portfolio demonstration. It is not intended for public commercial release or production deployment.
Security Warning: For your own security and privacy, do not use your real passwords (such as your personal email or banking passwords) when registering or testing accounts on this platform. Please use dummy or simulated credentials only.
